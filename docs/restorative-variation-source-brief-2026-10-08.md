# Restorative principles and individual variation

Checked 2026-10-08 for two short, non-instructional introductions. This is independent study material, not the friend's cohort method or educator review.

## M1-T14 · Restorative principles

- The public Joga Yoga 200-hour outline names restorative/yin principles and Yoga Nidra but gives no prop setup, class sequence, adaptation method or assessment standard.
- [Yoga Journal's Restorative Yoga overview](https://www.yogajournal.com/poses/types/restorative-types-of-yoga/) describes a small number of positions supported by props and time for rest. The lesson retains only **support** and **quiet** as memory cues. It avoids prescribed poses, durations, promised benefits and nervous-system claims.
- The soft forms and support layers are original abstract SVG. They are not a pose demonstration. The recall checks recognition of the supported/resting concept; the second prompt asks what to learn from the trainer.

## M2-T12 · Individual variation

- The school's public anatomy guide names the area, but cannot define the cohort's method.
- [OpenStax, Synovial Joints](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology/pages/9-4-synovial-joints) explains how joint shape, ligaments and adjacent structures affect available movement. It supports the lesson's structural distinction, not an estimate of any person's range.
- [Yoga Alliance RYS 200 standards](https://yogaalliance.org/Standards-for-RYS-Credentials) include awareness of physical limitations and adaptations in biomechanics. The lesson's “observe before you assume” teaching implication is an **inference** from the anatomy and that standard, not a quoted rule or school-specific cue.
- The two drawn arcs are schematic, unscaled and do not measure movement. No student assessment, stretch target, pose adjustment or health advice is supplied.

Both lessons have one concept-choice recall and one ungraded question for the trainer. The visible screens keep copy brief and detailed source/scope notes behind disclosure. A qualified teacher should review practical interpretation before any broader release.
