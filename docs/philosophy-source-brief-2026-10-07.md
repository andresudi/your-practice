## Scope for the first lesson

- One visual learning loop: **Text → Intention → Teaching**. The art is a conceptual memory aid, not a historical diagram or ritual instruction.
- The text view can say that Yoga Sūtra II.30 includes ahiṃsā among the yamas and that “non-harming” is a common translation. It must not quote extended passages or collapse the entire text into this one precept.
- The teaching view may invite the learner to notice choice, boundaries and consent. Ground explicit-consent claims in Yoga Alliance's current Code of Conduct; make no assertion that it is Joga Yoga's exact cohort policy.
- One factual recall question and one applied discrimination question can be scored. A personal reflection remains ungraded.

## Open review

A qualified yoga philosophy educator should review the framing, translation nuance, pronunciation and teaching application. The friend's instructor should confirm whether the cohort uses these texts or terms in the same way. That review has **not** happened.
