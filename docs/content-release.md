## Public curriculum mapping

Joga Yoga’s current 200-hour outline was checked on 2026-09-30. It explicitly includes asana fundamentals, human anatomy/physiology, traditional energy anatomy and application to practice. These foundation lessons map to M2-T01 (physical structures), M2-T03 (movement vocabulary), and M2-T15 (nervous system, a suggested subtopic from the school’s anatomy article). The three subtopics are our study organisation, not a verbatim cohort lesson schedule.

The outline does not supply an exhaustive required pose list, exact examination questions, or a cohort manual. Full curriculum coverage is not claimed. Benefits, contraindications, reviewed practical cues, and additional bodily systems remain future content.

## Diagram limits

The new local SVG diagrams are simplified, original vocabulary illustrations. Their geometry does not describe a prescribed range of movement. The nervous-system animation is a conceptual sensation–integration–response story, not a literal nerve pathway or a claim about physiological timing. The page labels these distinctions. Traditional energy concepts remain in a separate collection.

The existing Three.js Warrior II sequence and generated illustration remain marked as motion concepts pending educator review. They were not promoted to validated movement instruction.

## Study activities

Checked 2026-10-01. Four additional original activities are available: practice notebook organisation (M1), observing and revising classroom language (M3), respectful relationships reflection (M4), and rehearsal/feedback (M5). These activities use no prescriptive physical cues or breathing protocols.

- The Joga Yoga outline names posture, breathing and meditation; the practice activity helps organise notes under those categories. This is introductory study organisation, not instruction in the practices themselves.
- Teaching communication and observation are explicit outline subjects. The cue activity asks learners to record observations, compare wording, and seek teacher feedback.
- The ethics activity uses an original declined-activity scenario, informed by [Yoga Alliance’s Code of Conduct, principles 6 and 7](https://yogaalliance.org/policies-priorities-progress/code-of-conduct/). It invites reflection, not an automated judgement of a teacher’s ethics.
- Joga Yoga’s practicum includes teaching, observation, and feedback. The rehearsal activity turns those headings into a personal rehearsal routine, without claiming to reproduce the school’s assessment.

Each activity has an ungraded reflection with written guidance. No official exam questions or readiness score were invented. Source comparison checks curriculum relevance and factual claims; the teaching exercises are our original study suggestions, not school-endorsed course material.

Current release: **7 available introductory lessons/activities and 2 samples** (energy-framework context and Warrior II observation). The energy framework remains a sample because the cohort’s tradition/text has not been supplied. Practical pose art and animation remain unreviewed samples.

Topic counts indicate introductions, not mastery or exhaustive coverage. This completes the initial nine-lesson preview inventory; deeper curriculum lessons, a broader pose library, and educator-reviewed practice assets remain separate content work.
