## Source comparison

All sources below were read on 2026-10-02. This is source comparison by Codex, not educator review.

- Joga Yoga public 200-hour outline: Teaching Methodology explicitly lists Traditional Hatha, Creative Vinyasa, Ashtanga Vinyasa and Yin. Existing topics M1-T02 through M1-T05 are linked to the new introduction. This is public subject mapping, not a cohort timetable or examination blueprint.
- [YogaEasy / Emma Newlyn, What is Hatha Yoga?](https://www.yogaeasy.com/artikel/what-is-hatha-yoga): modern Hatha classes typically use slower postures and static holds; the traditional term has broader meaning. Do not interpret a class label as a guarantee of difficulty.
- [YogaEasy, Vinyasa Yoga](https://www.yogaeasy.com/artikel/vinyasa-yoga): breath-linked movement, variable sequences, pace and themes; slow flow is possible.
- [YogaEasy, Ashtanga Yoga](https://www.yogaeasy.com/artikel/ashtanga-yoga): established series, movement connected with breath and gradual learning. Distinguish the modern posture method from the broader eight-limb concept.
- [YogaEasy, Yin Yoga](https://www.yogaeasy.com/artikel/yin-yoga): longer-held passive poses, usually on the floor. No tissue, meridian, detoxification or health claims copied from the page.
